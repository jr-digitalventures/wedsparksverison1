# Policy draft research — 18 October 2026

Original drafting, not copied terms. Booking.com and competitor pages were used to identify subject areas, not to import their legal entity, payment role, fee model or operational promises.

- Booking.com privacy: https://www.booking.com/content/privacy.en-gb.html — account, communications, payment information, other people’s information and third-party processing.
- Booking.com terms: https://www.booking.com/content/terms.en-gb.html — platform/provider roles, upfront booking conditions, payment and cancellation disclosure.
- Easy Weddings privacy: https://www.easyweddings.com.au/privacy/ — wedding platform collection, purposes, suppliers and overseas providers.
- Wedshed terms: https://www.wedshed.com.au/terms-and-conditions — accounts, listing content and supplier enquiries. Its advertising model is different from WedSparks’ transaction model.
- OAIC: https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/your-privacy-rights — privacy rights and complaints.
- New Zealand Privacy Commissioner: https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/ — applicable principles and rights.
- ACCC contracts: https://www.accc.gov.au/consumers/buying-products-and-services/contracts — fair contract terms and rights.
- ACCC remedies: https://www.accc.gov.au/consumers/problem-with-a-product-or-service-you-bought/repair-replace-refund-cancel — consumer remedies cannot be replaced by an arbitrary platform cancellation rule.

## Confirm before effectiveness

Operator identity, registration and address; support/privacy contacts; applicable markets and currency; actual provider names, hosting countries, storage/cookie inventory and retention periods; credit availability/redemption/reversal handling; processor/payout arrangements; commission base, GST and processing charges; subscription renewal/cancellation/refund mechanics; complaint handling capacity and timing. No escrow, guaranteed supplier performance, fixed refund time, blanket forfeiture, automatic credit debt recovery or unlimited indemnity is promised.

Drafts describe the intended launch service and are visibly marked for review, noindex. A policy is not implementation: compare every clause against the live payment, privacy and account behaviour before launch.
